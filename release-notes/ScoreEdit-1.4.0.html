ScoreEdit now has its own About window with credits for the open source software it uses, and the Help menu opens the ABC 2.2 standard. The music engraving engine (CeolKit) has been updated from 1.9.0 to 1.9.1, which fixes where stems, flags and beams join.
  • A new About window shows the version and build number and links to the ScoreEdit website. Its Credits button opens the licenses of the open source software that ScoreEdit includes.
  • Help › ABC Standard v2.2 opens the ABC notation standard in its own window. It is included with the app, so it works offline.
  • Stems now meet their noteheads, flags meet their stems, and beams run to the outer edge of their first and last stems. Before, each of these joins was slightly offset. Grace notes are drawn the same way at their smaller size, and their stems are thinner to match.
