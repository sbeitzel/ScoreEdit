An engraving update. The music engraving engine (CeolKit) has been updated to 1.3.0, which teaches it to draw tunes in more than one voice — as aligned staves of a single system, or as two parts sharing one staff — and fixes a long list of layout problems around clefs, key changes, lyrics and tuplets. The editor, preview, and include handling are unchanged from 1.1.1.
  • Multi-voice tunes are engraved as one system of aligned staves instead of one voice after another. Voices keep the order they were declared in on their V: lines, and a voice declared only in the header is no longer silently dropped.
  • %%score and %%staves are honoured: they select which voices are drawn and in what order, group staves under braces and brackets, and say which bar lines run between staves. A %%score in the body takes effect where it appears, so the number of staves can change part-way through a tune.
  • Braces and brackets are drawn, stretched to the staves they join, with the indent they need reserved at the left margin; staves are spaced according to the group they belong to.
  • Voice names are printed in a gutter to the left of each system, from the name= and snm= settings on the V: line.
  • Two voices written onto the same staff are merged onto a common onset grid and drawn as two parts: stems opposed, rests offset, noteheads pulled apart where both voices land on the same spot, and beams, ties and slurs kept with the voice that opened them. V: stem= is honoured, the & voice overlay goes through the same path, and a floating voice's notes are assigned to a staff note by note.
  • Lyrics written on a w: line are engraved under the notes they belong to. Space had been reserved for them, but nothing was ever drawn.
  • Key signature, unit note length, and accidental bar memory are now scoped to the voice that set them — an accidental in one voice no longer alters the notes of another, and each voice keeps its own K: and L:.
  • A mid-tune K: now draws a key signature where the key changes, and systems that begin after the change open in the new key rather than the one the voice started in.
  • Clef fixes: a clef named on a K: line reaches the staff it applies to, the baritone clef is anchored on the third line instead of where a bass clef goes, and key signature accidentals are laid out for the clef the staff actually carries rather than at treble positions on every clef.
  • Inline [L:] and [M:] changes reach the renderer, so noteheads are sized and beams grouped against the values in force rather than the ones the voice opened with. A field reached through a \ line continuation now applies at the line break instead of after the joined line.
  • Variant ending brackets are drawn over the measures they cover; they had been parsed and then never engraved.
  • An incomplete or restarted tuplet keeps the notes it collected instead of silently discarding them, and a voice with no line in a line-set no longer drifts out of step with the tune's stave numbering.
  • Fixed a crash in the scan for file-global directives that could take down the preview before anything was drawn.
