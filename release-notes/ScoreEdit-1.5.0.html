The music engraving engine (CeolKit) has been updated from 1.9.1 to 2.1.0, which brings ScoreEdit's output much closer to abcm2ps. Because the default scale now matches abcm2ps, every tune engraves at a different size than before, and page breaks may move.
  • Font directives such as %%wordsfont, %%titlefont and %%setfont-1…4 now take effect, and the preview can use any font installed on your Mac. A monospaced font keeps lined-up columns lined up. $1…$4 switch fonts within a line of text.
  • %%scale, %%pagescale, %%stretchlast and %%stretchstaff work as they do in abcm2ps. With no %%scale, music is drawn at abcm2ps's default of 0.75; write %%scale 1 in the file header to get the old size back.
  • Notes are placed for the clef in force, so bass, alto and other clefs are correct. Clef changes part way through a tune are drawn, a K: can name a clef without a key, and octave= takes effect.
  • W: words print below the tune, and a tune with several C: (or other repeated header) lines keeps them all.
  • Chord symbols draw proper ♭ and ♯ signs, and note spacing makes room for chord symbols and annotations so they no longer overlap.
  • Only notes shorter than a quarter are beamed, every stem in a beam group points the same way, and a note on the middle line follows the stem before it.
  • Repeat signs that fall on a line break are split across it, the last line of a page no longer prints over the footer, and the last line of a tune is stretched or spaced like the line above it.
